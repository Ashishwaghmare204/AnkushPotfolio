About

More Than a Mechanic.
A Problem Solver.

I started my journey working on conventional two-wheelers — petrol bikes and scooters. Over several years of hands-on work, I built a strong foundation in mechanical systems, engine troubleshooting, and electrical wiring.

As electric scooters became common, I moved into EV servicing and diagnosis. I currently work at a TVS EV service center, where I deal with real-world EV scooter problems every day — battery issues, charging faults, motor and controller diagnostics, wiring problems, and more.

My approach is simple: understand the actual problem before replacing parts. I believe in honest diagnosis and practical solutions — not guesswork.
